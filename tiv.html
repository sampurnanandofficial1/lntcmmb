Analysis 5 · Key Patterns — What the Numbers Reveal
Pattern 1
Concentration
Top 4 OEMs (TH+JCB+HYU+SANY) = 68.0%. Premium tier (KOM+CAT+KOB+VOL) = 22.3%. Komatsu already holds 30.7% of the premium sub-market — the de facto premium leader. Strategic question: grow the premium tier, not enter it.
Pattern 2
Growth Vectors
In a +27.4% market, share is moving — 3 of top 5 are swapping share. SANY: +2.57pp · Hyundai: +2.50pp. Tata Hitachi losing −1.59pp. Komatsu: flat 7.0% (−0.01pp) — only top-5 tracking industry growth exactly. Defending share, not gaining it.
Pattern 3 — Highlight
Geographic Identity
Komatsu is 1.51× more state-concentrated than industry — different geography from peers. Top 3 states = 40.4% of Komatsu vs 30.3% industry. Mining/coal/aggregate economies over-index. Highway/infra states favour price-led OEMs.
Pattern 4
Stronghold Signature
Every state where Komatsu over-indexes ≥1.5× national is a mining/coal/aggregate economy: Karnataka 2.48× · NE States 1.86× · WB&Sikkim 1.55×. Near-zero overlap with highway/infra megaproject corridors.
Pattern 5
Volume vs Value
Gujarat = industry #1 state (10.4% of TIV). Komatsu only #6 there (4.15%). Gap to leader = 16.2pp. Closing this gap would lift national share by +1.7pp — a structural move worth more than incremental wins in current strongholds.
Strategic Takeaway
The Play Forward
Deepen mining/aggregate strongholds where Komatsu already over-indexes 1.55–2.48×. Alternatively, compete in highway/infra geographies where SANY (+2.57pp) and Hyundai (+2.50pp) are absorbing growth at the expense of the share-bleeding leader (Tata Hitachi, −1.59pp).
