Hours adjustment: −5% per 1,000 hrs above 2,000-hr baseline (range 3–7%; default 5%) | Source: Heavy Equipment Appraisal
Hours sweet spot for resale: 1,000–3,000 hrs. Red flags: >8,000 hrs (overhaul decision point); >12,000 hrs (flat-tail / export tier).
Step 2 — Condition Multipliers
Undercarriage: Full UC replacement cost PC210 = ₹15–25L (India, OEM quality). Deduct full cost if <30% remaining; pro-rate above. | Source: IndiaMART / Sevenstar / Quipli
Engine: Oil analysis wear metals (KOWA program) + blow-by test. High iron/silicon = 30–40% shorter projected life at same meter hours. | Source: MEVAS; Huaying Machinery
Hydraulics: Leaks or sluggish cycles = −20%+ (Quipli). Contaminated fluid = add repair + decontamination cost. | Source: Quipli; Plant & Equipment
Structure: Frame cracks / major weld repairs = pass/fail gate. Cosmetics ±5–10%. | Source: Boleo; Quipli
KOMTRAX idle: <25% = good; 25–40% = normal; >40% = high idle penalty. Normal idle for 36T excavator ~34–40% (AEM). | Source: AEM telematics report
Fault history: Recurring powertrain/hydraulic codes = −8–12%; isolated sensor codes = minor. | Source: FairTrade Machinery; Komatsu fault-code library
Step 3 — Service & Documentation
Full OEM records + MCP: +8% (verified care reduces buyer risk, supports higher ask). | Source: L&T Annual Review 2025; L&T MCP-5 program
MCP-5 warranty active: +5–8% (transferable 5yr/12,500hr powertrain warranty on PC205/PC210-10M0). | Source: Komatsu India / L&T Feb 2024
Documentation complete: Form 22, RC book, original invoice, NOC/Form 35 (hypothecation clearance), valid insurance = +3%. Encumbered title = −5 to −10%+ or sale blocked. | Source: Tata Capital; TaxGuru GST-on-CE guide
Single owner: +3% vs 2-owner; 3+ owners −5%. | Source: Jumbobee; Niboka machinery guide
