Key Insights & Strategic Analysis
What the Top 5 OEM numbers reveal about Komatsu's competitive position
Pattern 1
Concentration
Top 4 OEMs (Tata Hitachi + JCB + Hyundai + SANY) = 68.0% of total market. Premium tier where Komatsu competes = 22.3%. Komatsu already holds 30.7% of the premium sub-market — the de facto premium leader. Strategic question: grow the premium tier, not enter it.
Pattern 2
Growth Vectors
In a +27.4% market, share is moving. SANY: +2.57 percentage points (+65.6% growth). Hyundai: +2.50 percentage points (+47.9%). Tata Hitachi bleeding −1.59 percentage points. Komatsu: flat (−0.01 percentage points) at exactly industry growth rate — the only top-5 OEM neither gaining nor losing share.
Pattern 3 — Key Insight
Geographic Identity
Komatsu is 1.51× more state-concentrated than industry. Top 3 states = 40.4% of Komatsu volume vs 30.3% for industry. Every state where Komatsu over-indexes ≥1.5× national is a mining/coal/aggregate economy. Highway/infra states favour price-led OEMs.
Pattern 4
Stronghold Signature
Karnataka 2.48× national · NE States 1.86× · WB&Sikkim 1.55×. Zero overlap with NHAI highway mega-projects. Komatsu is a mining/aggregate-economy OEM competing in a market dominated by highway/infra demand.
Pattern 5
Volume vs Value Gap
Gujarat = 10.4% of national TIV (India's #1 state). Komatsu only 4.15% there — Hyundai 20.4%, Tata 19.6% dominate. Gap to leader = 16.2pp. Closing this gap = +1.7 percentage points national share — a structural move worth more than deepening current strongholds.
Strategic Takeaway
The Play Forward
Two paths: (A) Deepen mining strongholds where Komatsu over-indexes 1.55–2.48× — lower competitive intensity, sustainable. (B) Contest highway/infra geographies where SANY (+2.57 percentage points) and Hyundai (+2.50 percentage points) are absorbing Tata Hitachi's declining share — higher volume but price-led competition. Both are valid; only (A) plays to Komatsu's premium identity.
